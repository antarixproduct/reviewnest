# ReviewNest Support Terms

Support depends on the purchase tier or written agreement.

## Included Basic Support

Basic setup support may include:

- Clarifying the setup guide
- Helping identify missing environment variables
- Checking obvious deployment configuration issues
- Explaining MongoDB Atlas, Render, and Gmail SMTP setup steps

## Not Included Unless Purchased Separately

The following are custom services:

- Deploying the app for the buyer
- Custom branding
- Custom domains
- Code customization
- Feature development
- Database migration
- Long-term maintenance
- Bug fixes caused by buyer modifications
- Third-party account troubleshooting beyond basic guidance

## Buyer Responsibilities

The buyer is responsible for:

- Their Render account
- Their MongoDB Atlas account
- Their Gmail account and app password
- Their Google review link
- Their WhatsApp usage
- Backups
- Legal compliance for customer contact and review requests

## Recommended Paid Services

You may offer:

- Setup service
- Branding service
- Custom domain setup
- Monthly maintenance
- Feature customization
- Priority support

Suggested monthly support range: USD 19-49 or INR 999-2999 per business.
